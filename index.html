FAQ

Who holds my money?

Your deposit sits in the vault account on ApeX Omni. The manager can trade with it but cannot withdraw it to their own wallet.

How do I withdraw?

On the vault page choose “Withdraw” and redeem your shares. The USDT value at the current NAV returns to your ApeX account.

What does the manager earn?

A percentage of the profit you make (shown under Vault terms). If you don’t profit, the manager earns nothing from you.

Is there a minimum deposit?

Minimums are set by ApeX and shown on the deposit screen of the vault page.
